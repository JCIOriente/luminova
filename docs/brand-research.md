# JCI & JCI Oriente — Brand Research & Design Brief

Compiled research feeding the design phase for `apps/spotlight` (the public marketing site for JCI Oriente). Source: JCI's official **Brand Guidelines v1.2 (November 27, 2025)**, published in preparation for the 2026 global rollout, plus chapter and national-org references.

---

## 1. What JCI is

**Junior Chamber International (JCI)** is a non-profit, worldwide federation of young leaders and entrepreneurs (members typically 18–40). Over **200,000 members across 100+ countries**. Founded 1915; celebrated its 110th anniversary at the November 2025 World Congress in Tunisia, where the new logo and brand identity were unveiled.

### Mission
> Provide leadership development opportunities that empower young people to create positive change.

### Vision
> To be the leading global network of young active citizens.

### Four Areas of Opportunity (the structural pillars)
1. **Individual Development** — personal growth, skills, confidence
2. **Community Action** — local projects with real impact
3. **International Cooperation** — cross-border collaboration
4. **Business & Entrepreneurship** — career and venture development

### Brand Story (verbatim from the 2026 guidelines)
> The world needs people who don't wait for change — but create it. Young generations everywhere share a common drive: to grow, to lead, and to make a difference that truly matters. Turning that drive into real impact takes more than ambition. It takes opportunity, perspective, and a community that believes in your potential.
>
> JCI offers a global network of emerging leaders who learn by doing, grow by sharing, and lead by acting. Across business, culture, and community, our members step up to challenge and improve themselves and each other — amplifying the skills, confidence, and connections to turn ideas into action. That's how we help develop leaders for a changing world.
>
> At JCI, you don't join another network. You join a worldwide movement with more than a century of proven heritage — combining global reach, training opportunities, and an entrepreneurial mindset to empower leadership and a true sense of belonging.
>
> **Become the Change.**

### Core Messages (the three repeatable hooks)
- **Focus on Real Impact** — "We do what we do in order to create meaningful impact on our members and communities."
- **Developing Leadership Skills** — "Your engagement results in becoming better through learning by doing."
- **Diversity of Opportunities** — "Our 4 areas of opportunity provide a wide array of growth possibilities and a purposeful sense of belonging."

## 2. New JCI Visual Identity (2026)

### 2.1 Logo
The 2025 rebrand (developed with Pavlov Brand Consultancy, informed by the JCI Youth Leadership Survey) modernised the shield while retaining the historical blue palette and shield silhouette. Key meaning encoded in the mark:

| Element | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Shield | Legacy & heritage (used for 100+ years) |
| Concentric ripple | Impact & personal development radiating outward |
| Embedded J / C / I letters | Ownable, recognisable acronym |
| Four shield elements | The 4 global regions / 4 Areas of Opportunity |

**Construction:** three sets of concentric circles, geometrically aligned. Never alter, rotate, stretch, recolour, outline, add effects, or change spacing between elements.

**Logo variants:**
- **Primary** — shield + JCI wordmark (default for most uses)
- **Secondary** — primary + full "Junior Chamber International" name (formal/admin documents, first introductions)
- **National / Local** — primary logo + location name underneath ("Oriente" or "Bolivia – Oriente")
- **Shield icon only** — favicons, social profile pictures, small merchandise

**Local chapter layout rules** (directly applicable to JCI Oriente):
- Location name typeface: **Plus Jakarta Sans Bold**, proper case, **JCI Blue** by default
- At 570px logo width, location text is **60pt** with 110% leading, 0 tracking
- Always **left-aligned** to the logo
- Vertically aligned to the **bottom of the shield**, no gap
- "Oriente" fits comfortably on the first line under the shield

**Clearspace:** minimum equal to half the shield width on every side. Minimum size: 5mm print / **32px digital**.

### 2.2 Colour Palette

#### Primary
| Name | HEX | RGB | CMYK | PMS |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **JCI Blue** | `#0097D7` | 0 / 151 / 215 | 77 / 25 / 0 / 0 | 2925 C |
| **JCI Black** | `#130F2D` | 19 / 15 / 45 | 100 / 98 / 47 / 66 | 5395 C |
| **JCI White** | `#FFFFFF` | 255 / 255 / 255 | 0 / 0 / 0 / 0 | — |

> Note: JCI Black is intentionally a very dark navy/indigo (`#130F2D`), not true black. This matters for the web — use it as the body text and dark-mode background colour rather than `#000000`.

#### Secondary
| Name | HEX | RGB | CMYK | PMS |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **JCI Navy** | `#1F4789` | 31 / 71 / 137 | 96 / 76 / 15 / 3 | 2728 C |
| **JCI Teal** | `#57BCBC` | 87 / 188 / 188 | 63 / 0 / 30 / 0 | 3265 C |
| **JCI Yellow** | `#EFC40F` | 239 / 196 / 15 | 7 / 21 / 93 / 0 | 142 C |

Tints at 25%, 50%, 75% are sanctioned for all colours.

#### Colour hierarchy (approximate distribution per layout)
- JCI Blue / Navy / Teal / Black / White make up the bulk (~95%)
- **JCI Yellow** is an accent only — used sparingly (~5%) to emphasise CTAs, key stats, highlighted moments. Do not turn it into a section background or large block.

#### Pairing rule
Always prioritise legibility. Avoid low-contrast pairings (e.g., Teal text on Blue background, Yellow on White).

### 2.3 Typography

#### Primary typeface — **Plus Jakarta Sans**
Available free on Google Fonts: `https://fonts.google.com/specimen/Plus+Jakarta+Sans`. Weights used: Light, Regular, SemiBold, Bold.

Tone: "contemporary sans-serif… balance of professionalism, modernity, and approachability." General guidance: **lean toward lighter weights** (Light / Regular / Medium) for body and large text; reserve Bold for emphasis, key messages, and callouts.

#### Type scale (from the official guidelines — translate to web)
| Role | Weight | Size | Leading | Tracking |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Title (H1) | Regular | 48pt | 120% | 0 |
| Subtitle (H2) | Regular | 24pt | 130% | 0 |
| Subheading (H3) | SemiBold | 20pt | 140% | 0 |
| Paragraph | Regular | 16pt | 140% | 0 |
| Quote | Arvo Regular | 24pt | 130% | 0 |

#### Secondary typeface — **Arvo** (slab serif)
`https://fonts.google.com/specimen/Arvo`. Use **only for large pull-quotes and editorial callouts**. Do not use for body, navigation, or buttons.

#### Fallback — **Readex Pro**
Reserved for Canva-without-Pro situations. Do **not** use on the website. Keep Plus Jakarta Sans as the actual font stack.

### 2.4 The Ripple — JCI's central graphic motif

The concentric-circle "ripple" extracted from the logo is JCI's **primary graphic element** across the entire identity system. It is the visual language for backgrounds, section dividers, hero illustrations, transitions, and accents.

**Construction rules** (use these when generating SVG / animated ripples):
- A series of **concentric circles**, each ring split into **four equal quarters** (small gap between quarters)
- Gap between quarters = ring width
- Spacing between rings = ring width
- **Each ring is rotated 15° relative to the next ring inward** — this is what gives the motif subtle dynamic movement
- Can be expanded outward indefinitely by adding rings using the same spacing/line-width rules

The ripple represents "impact radiating from the individual outward" — directly mapping to the brand promise. Use it liberally but cohesively; avoid mixing other geometric motifs.

---

### 2.5 Photography Direction

When sourcing or shooting hero / section imagery:
- **People at the centre** — faces, expressions, interactions
- **Candid, not staged** — real moments over polished portraits
- **Diversity & representation** — global, inclusive feel
- **Energy & optimism** — bright, natural light, open compositions

For a Bolivian chapter site this means: prioritise authentic photos of Oriente members at projects (World Clean Up Day, Madre Emprendedora, etc.) over stock imagery. If stock is needed as placeholder, choose images that match the energy/diversity criteria.

---

### 2.6 Logo placement on layouts
- **Preferred:** top-left or top-right (aligns with reading patterns)
- **Optional:** bottom-left or bottom-right
- **Last resort:** top-centre / bottom-centre
- Always respect clearspace = ½ shield width.

## 5. Design Direction Summary (input for `frontend-design` → `ui-ux-pro-max`)

**Tone:** institutional, modern, energetic, optimistic. Confident but not corporate-stiff. Should feel like a global movement with local roots — heritage (110 years) meets a young (18–40) audience.

**Aesthetic anchors:**
- **Bright JCI Blue (`#0097D7`)** is the dominant accent — generous use as primary action colour, link colour, and brand surface
- **Deep JCI Black `#130F2D`** as the strong dark surface — use it confidently for hero backgrounds, footers, contrasting sections (not pure black)
- **Crisp white space** between sections — the rebrand's whole point is clarity and authenticity, not density
- **Ripple motif as connective tissue** — use it as ambient background patterns, section transitions, decorative accents, loading states. Treat it the way Stripe uses its gradient, or Linear uses its grid
- **Plus Jakarta Sans throughout**, leaning on Light/Regular for large text and SemiBold/Bold for emphasis — modern hierarchy, lots of breathing room, generous line-height (140% body, 120% titles)
- **Yellow only as a true accent** — single CTA highlights, key stats, never as a section background
- **Photography is real and human-centred** — leave room in layouts for full-bleed candid photos of Oriente members in action

**Avoid:**
- Generic chamber-of-commerce / civic-org templates with stock handshakes and rotating banners
- Saturated multi-colour gradients (the palette is intentionally restrained)
- Decorative shapes other than the ripple
- Heavy bold weights as the default body — guidelines explicitly say lean lighter

**Localisation:** all copy in Spanish (Bolivian register). Keep "Become the Change" untranslated as the brand tagline if used, but pair it with a Spanish equivalent ("Sé el cambio" / "Conviértete en el cambio").

**Accessibility (validation pass with `ui-ux-pro-max`):**
- JCI Blue on white: contrast ~3.4:1 — **passes for large text and UI components, NOT for body text**. Use JCI Black for body copy on white, JCI Blue for links/buttons/headings only — or use JCI Navy (`#1F4789`) where Blue would fail contrast.
- JCI Yellow has very low contrast on white — never use for text; only as a background fill with dark text on top.
- Provide focus styles using JCI Blue with a visible offset ring.
