## What is already settled — do not re-litigate

The user made three binding product decisions, and both documents have been through a **fact-check
pass** (every `file:line` citation verified against this worktree) and an **adversarial design
review** (2 Critical, 5 High, 10 Medium findings, all reconciled into the docs). The decisions:

- **D1** — our own single-use invite token in Firestore, not Firebase's `generatePasswordResetLink`
  oobCode.
- **D2** — `/forgot-password` is deleted. All recovery is operator-mediated.
- **D3** — recovery for an already-provisioned member is available to any `create:MemberLogin`
  holder, not Admin-only. The user was told this hands delegates an impersonation primitive over
  ordinary members and chose it. The power-seat, direct-grants and `accountIsPrivileged` guards
  contain it to grant-free members.

Motivation, so you keep it in view: Firebase's transactional email is unbrandable, its links route
through `firebaseapp.com`, and it lands in spam. The chapter coordinates over WhatsApp. **No Firebase
email anywhere in the auth flow.**

If you believe a settled decision is wrong, say so in one paragraph and keep implementing — do not
stop and do not silently substitute your own.

## The five things most likely to go wrong

Each was found by review, not by the original plan. They are the reason the plan reads the way it
does.

1. **`provision-gate.ts` must change (plan step 9).** `memberProvisionBlocked`'s `hasLogin` conjunct
   is a *mount* gate in `member-row-menu.tsx`. Skip it and beacon allows recovery while the UI never
   offers it — D3 ships as dead code. Only the `hasLogin` conjunct moves.
2. **The parity test passes vacuously (step 8).** `clientOffersInvite ⟹ beaconGuardsAllow` is
   trivially true when the client offers nothing. It must also assert the offered set is **non-empty
   for the D3 principal**.
3. **Revoke + mint + project must be ONE batch (step 5).** A partial failure leaves a live token the
   operator already sent, with the projection pointing at the old hash — and nothing can ever revoke
   it. Assert the single `commit()` in a test.
4. **The privilege guards re-run at redemption (step 6).** The token outlives the authorization
   decision by up to 48 h. Without the re-check, a delegate keeps a link and redeems it after the
   member is promoted. Exempt `issuedByAdmin: true`.
5. **`routeTree.gen.ts` is generated by vite, not vitest (steps 12, 13a, 13b).** Build before you run
   vitest, and keep 13a/13b split — the generated tree imports the routes 13b deletes, so a combined
   step cannot typecheck at its midpoint and `pre-commit.sh` will block the commit.
